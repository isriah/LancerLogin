Plain-language notice

Privacy boundaries

Each installation belongs to the organization that runs it. Its data stays in that organization's own Cloudflare account and kiosks.

Stored by your installation

  • Organization settings, accounts, roster, meetings, attendance, corrections, reports, and audit records, in your D1 database
  • Data for any plugins you turn on, such as outreach hours or carpool addresses, in the same database
  • Encrypted integration credentials; saved values are never displayed again
  • On each Raspberry Pi kiosk: its pairing, the links between members and fingerprints, badges, or QR codes, a cache of active members, and scans waiting to be sent
  • On each web kiosk, in that device's browser: its pairing, a cache of active members, and scans waiting to be sent
  • Member PINs, only as salted hashes: in your database, in entire-installation backups, and on kiosks that require a PIN
  • Fingerprint templates inside the R503 reader where they were enrolled, unless an Admin turns on cloud storage

Not collected or shared

  • No usage data is sent to the LancerLogin project or anyone else
  • Raw fingerprint images are never captured or uploaded
  • Raw badge and QR values never leave the kiosk; it keeps only keyed digests
  • Network addresses are not stored; sign-in rate limits keep only a keyed hash for a day

Integrations you turn on send only what they need: Google Calendar and Discord receive meeting titles, notes, and times; Resend and Discord deliver the messages you configure. The FIRST connection sends FIRST only the team number being looked up, with your saved FIRST username and token for FRC and FTC; it sends nothing about your members or guests.

What a kiosk receives

Every paired kiosk receives the names and member IDs of your active members, so it can recognize them without a network. Some settings send more:

  • Member search also sends each member's email address to that kiosk, because members can search by the start of their email. The kiosk never shows emails; results list names and member IDs only.
  • Require PIN also sends each member's PIN hash to that kiosk, so it can check PINs while offline. The PIN itself is never stored anywhere. The hashes are removed from the kiosk when Require PIN is turned off or the kiosk is unpaired.
  • Discord codes are checked by the server only. A code works once, expires after 2 minutes, and is never stored on a kiosk or in a backup.

A 4 to 6 digit PIN is short, so someone who copies a PIN hash from a stolen device could work the PIN out. Treat a PIN as a way to stop members signing in for each other, not as a password.

Web kiosks and trust

Pairing gives a kiosk its own credential. On a web kiosk that credential is kept in the device's browser, and the settings PIN only locks the kiosk's menu on screen; it does not protect the credential. A paired kiosk is trusted to record visits, including visits it checked while offline, and to set or reset member PINs from its maintenance menu. Pair web kiosks only on laptops and tablets your team controls, and choose Retire kiosk on Kiosks at once if a device is lost.

Optional fingerprint cloud storage

Fingerprint cloud storage is off by default and only an Admin can turn it on, in Settings › Modules › Attendance › Fingerprint sharing. Get your organization's permission first: fingerprint templates are biometric data.

When it is on, new enrollments upload to your installation, where the server manages a separate 32-byte encryption key and can decrypt templates for authorized paired kiosks. This is not end-to-end private from the server. Templates are briefly in memory while they are encrypted or delivered, and are not intentionally stored in plain form in D1, files, or logs. New kiosks can receive enrollments even while every earlier kiosk is offline.

Only enrollments made while a kiosk is online carry a verified link to the member. Older or offline enrollments require re-enrollment before sharing. Unknown reader slots are never read or overwritten.

Cloud copies are deleted when the member is made inactive or their member ID changes, when cloud storage or Attendance is turned off, or when the installation is restored from a backup. Turning cloud storage off does not erase templates already on readers. To erase a member's template from a reader, remove that member's fingerprint link in Authentication maintenance on the kiosk, then use Erase unlinked fingerprints for that kiosk in the dashboard.

Reset all fingerprint mappings removes the links and the cloud copies, but not the templates on the readers. After a reset, scanning one of those fingers no longer identifies anyone, and the kiosk does not upload them. Erase unlinked fingerprints then erases only the templates that were never shared. It skips templates the kiosk had saved to or received from cloud storage, and those stay in that reader's own storage. One is overwritten when you enroll a new finger into its slot with Replace occupied slot.

Backups

Portable dashboard backups exclude fingerprint templates, sharing settings, and the encryption key. They do include password hashes, member PIN hashes, encrypted integration secrets, and attendance history, so protect them like an Admin password. Raw database exports and copies of a kiosk's local files are more sensitive still and may contain information already deleted from the live installation.

Retiring or repurposing a kiosk

Retiring a kiosk in the dashboard stops its credential working, but it cannot erase the device. Before giving a web kiosk's laptop or tablet away, unpair it from its maintenance menu or clear the site's data in its browser. Before giving a Raspberry Pi kiosk away or reusing it, remove its member links in Authentication maintenance on the kiosk and wipe or replace its SD card.

Deletion and contact

Data stays in your installation until an Admin deletes it in Settings › Data. Exporting a report or backup creates a copy and does not remove anything.

Send privacy and security questions to robolancers@gmail.com. Do not include credentials, attendance records, or backups. Community support is best-effort with no response-time commitment. The full privacy reference covers each boundary in detail.
