# Outreach activities and participation

Outreach keeps staff-created activities separate from the hours members actually submit. Members do not need dashboard accounts. They normally submit through the public participation form or a linked Discord account. Staff can add a manual entry when needed, with a reason retained alongside the entry.

## Configure Outreach

1. Enable Outreach in **Settings > Modules** and complete its setup before inviting members to submit.
2. Choose the reporting rules and public submission options appropriate for your organization.
3. Open **Outreach > Settings** or **Settings > Modules > Outreach**. **General** contains reporting defaults, publication and privacy settings. **Supported teams** and **Labels** contain searchable, paginated lists. Active records appear by default; change the status filter to find archived records. Use **Add team**, **Add label**, or a row's **Edit** action to open its form.
4. Leave automatic activity publication off unless you want events shared to configured destinations. Calendar and Discord connections are configured separately in Integrations.

Public form privacy is configured by an Administrator in **Outreach > Settings** or **Settings > Modules > Outreach**. Live member-ID validation is off by default. **Validate ID only** confirms whether the exact entered ID belongs to an active member. **Validate ID and show name** additionally shows the matching member name. Both reveal personal information and should be enabled only when appropriate for the installation. Checks run after leaving the ID field; no roster list is downloaded or cached in the public browser.

Members request a correction by entering their member ID, choosing an activity, and describing the issue. They do not need a receipt reference and cannot browse participation history. An archived or older activity remains available for correction; future activities are not listed.

Labels organize activities without changing how members report their time. An activity may have several labels or none. Team support uses a supported team; an Event or Other service activity does not require one.

## Create and find activities

Open **Outreach > Activities** and choose **Create activity**. Select its type, service date and title. Description, location and labels provide optional context.

**Event times** are optional start and end values. Leave both blank for an all-day event, or enter both to plan times in the installation time zone. Google Calendar receives untimed events as all-day dates; Discord represents them with the installation's full local day. Planned times do not change members' recorded participation. Automatic publication remains a separate installation setting, off by default.

The calendar and table show scheduled events by default. **Filter** can include Team support and Other service. The Activity selector remains available for opening an activity outside the visible calendar range or types.

## Review an activity's hours

Select an activity from the calendar, table or Activity selector. Its detail view shows the activity heading, summary, and participation records. Use browser Back to return to the collection, or open Outreach from the primary navigation to choose another activity or inspect the Submission Log.

**Logged participation** shows each member's name and total hours. **Details** expands the individual entries, showing actual times, duration, source, revision, notes and any staff reason. Each counted entry has **Edit** and **Delete** controls. Staff provide a reason for either action and explicitly confirm deletion. Deletion excludes the entry from totals and reports while retaining its original submission and immutable revision history. Deleted entries remain visible in Details. Records are loaded through every page before totals appear; a failed page does not show an incomplete total. Refresh the browser page to retry or load newer participation.

Use **Edit activity** to change an activity's type, supported team, title, date, planned Event times, description, location, labels, impact relevance, and archive status. Existing participation dates, times, and hours remain as submitted. Converting an Event to another type removes its planned Event schedule and queues removal of any previously published provider event. Team support requires a supported team. **Add member participation** is in the Logged participation card. The details card includes reporting status and an icon for each configured publication destination. Click a muted unpublished icon to publish there. A published icon has full color and a check mark; clicking it opens **Update** and **Unpublish** actions for that destination. Queued and failed deliveries show their own status. Publication does not change participation hours. Reporting follows the installation's configured default. **Reopen for 24 hours** appears when reporting has closed or fewer than 24 hours remain, and sets a deadline 24 hours from that action. Activities have no custom reporting-deadline editor.

Correction requests appear in the activity when needed. For a request associated with a member and activity rather than a specific submission, staff select the affected submission before correcting or voiding it. Only that member's matching submissions are offered. Staff review notes and entry revisions retain the decision history.

**Submission Log** provides paginated entry history ordered by the time each entry was logged, newest first. Editing an entry does not change its original logged position. Use Reports for cross-activity totals and analysis.

## Submit participation

The public **submit-hours** form begins with no activity or times selected. Choose an eligible dated activity and explicitly select start and end times. Enter in a form field does not submit; use **Record completed participation**. Enter remains available for new lines in notes and keyboard activation of the submit button. After recording an entry, **Record separate participation** resets activity and time choices.
