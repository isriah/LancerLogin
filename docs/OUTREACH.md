# Outreach activities and participation

Outreach keeps staff-created activities separate from the hours members actually submit. Members do not need dashboard accounts. They normally submit through the public participation form or a linked Discord account. Staff can add a manual entry when needed, with a reason retained alongside the entry.

## Configure Outreach

1. Enable Outreach in **Settings > Modules** and complete its setup before inviting members to submit.
2. Choose the reporting rules and public submission options appropriate for your organization.
3. Add supported teams and reusable Outreach labels from **Outreach > Settings** or the Outreach module settings in the primary Settings menu. Creation opens from **Add team** or **Add label**.
4. Leave automatic activity publication off unless you want scheduled events shared to configured destinations. Calendar and Discord connections are configured separately in Integrations.

Public form privacy is configured by an Administrator in **Outreach > Settings** or **Settings > Modules > Outreach > Outreach settings**. Live member-ID validation is off by default. **Validate ID only** confirms whether the exact entered ID belongs to an active member. **Validate ID and show name** additionally shows the matching member name. Both reveal personal information and should be enabled only when appropriate for the installation. Checks run after leaving the ID field; no roster list is downloaded or cached in the public browser.

Members request a correction by entering their member ID, choosing an activity, and describing the issue. They do not need a receipt reference and cannot browse participation history. An archived or older activity remains available for correction; future activities are not listed.

Labels organize activities without changing how members report their time. An activity may have several labels or none. Team support uses a supported team; an Event or Other service activity does not require one.

## Create and find activities

Open **Outreach > Activities** and choose **Create activity**. Select its type, service date and title. Description, location and labels provide optional context.

**Set event times** adds the Event's planned start and end times. These are not a publication date or a member's recorded hours. They are optional when saving an activity, but required before sharing an Event to Google Calendar or Discord. Automatic publication remains a separate installation setting, off by default.

The calendar and table show scheduled events by default. **Visibility** can include Team support and Other service. The Activity selector remains available for opening an activity outside the visible calendar range or types.

## Review an activity's hours

Select an activity from the calendar, table or Activity selector. Its detail view uses the same navigation, heading, summary and record-card layout as a meeting. Use **Switch activity** to inspect another activity or **Back to Activities** to return to the collection.

**Logged participation** has one row per member and totals only currently counted entries. **View submissions** expands the individual submissions, showing actual times, duration, source, revision, notes and any staff reason. Void submissions remain visible for review but are excluded from hours and counted-entry totals. Records are loaded through every page before totals appear; a failed page does not show an incomplete total. Use **Refresh participation** to retry or refresh the activity.

Activity details retain event-time and label editing, reporting-window controls, and Event publication status. **Publish** or **Resend** shares a scheduled Event to the configured destinations; it does not change participation hours. A closed activity can be explicitly reopened for reporting recovery.

Correction requests appear in the activity when needed. For a request associated with a member and activity rather than a specific submission, staff select the affected submission before correcting or voiding it. Only that member's matching submissions are offered. Staff review notes and entry revisions retain the decision history.

**Submission Log** provides the wider entry history. Use Reports for cross-activity totals and analysis.
