# Completed validation generation reuse

WU188 permits the pinned independent validation database to serve a later backup after its previous replay completed. It never deletes application or updater-state data, creates/deletes cloud resources, expires ownership, or recovers an orphan validation.

The existing replay constructor accepts optional trusted withClosedEpoch. Initial empty-target replay remains compatible without it; reuse requires it. createVerifiedBackup passes the same helper used for capture. Every cleanup advance checks the new sealed capture's exact job/epoch and schema ledger against live authority. The new job must differ from the prior capture job and its epoch must be strictly later. The validation binding remains exclusive to this runtime; these guards do not authorize unrelated privileged writers.

The fixed target tables _ll_snapshot_owner and _ll_snapshot_steps remain in place. Step numbers increase across generations; old step receipts are never reset/deleted. A third fixed table, _ll_snapshot_generations, records identity, prior owner, status and completed final step. Replay marks the target complete before committing its encrypted completion record. This final marker is an idempotent exact-generation seal guarded by owner, snapshot, identity and final step; a lost seal acknowledgment repeats no data mutation. Both records and the exact owner/step must agree before reuse. Historical completed replay reads remain bound to their encrypted receipt and never claim or inspect a newer target generation.

Existing completed targets without generation metadata have a checked adoption path. It requires the old encrypted replay completion with no pending operation, exact target owner/final step, complete old compiled schema and retained sealed capture. The old catalog is selected from the static registry by its recorded digest; the new capture accessor is never substituted. Adoption first records encrypted intent; its guarded transaction creates generation metadata and an exact step receipt. A missing acknowledgment is reconciled without repeating CREATE. Incomplete/rejected/unknown targets are refused.

A new generation first claims an encrypted transition and then atomically records a receipt and transfers target ownership. Every cleanup batch similarly records pending intent before dispatch, inserts an exact owner/snapshot/step receipt in the same transaction as changes, and increments the target counter. The next advance reads that receipt before changing the encrypted cursor. Missing receipt stays unknown; it never permits a new dispatch. Lost acknowledgments reconcile the original identity.

Cleanup derives names only from the prior reviewed catalog/acyclic replay plan. It drops known triggers in batches of at most eight, deletes at most 64 rows per call in reverse foreign-key order, then drops empty child tables before parents in batches of at most eight. Parent uniqueness indexes remain until their tables are dropped. Foreign keys remain enabled. sqlite_sequence cannot be dropped: its exact canonical schema is retained and any remaining contents are cleared in bounded batches. Only exact lifecycle metadata plus empty sqlite_sequence may remain when the target becomes active for the next replay.

All historical encrypted snapshots and replay records remain intact, along with target step/generation receipts. No automatic retention deletion is added. Receipt growth needs separate future compaction policy. Cleanup is resumable but not instantly atomic as a whole: the target may be partially cleaned, owned exclusively by the new generation, until all bounded steps finish. The old validated snapshot remains retained independently throughout.
