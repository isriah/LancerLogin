# LancerLogin v0.15.0 - Meeting detail, integration controls, and dashboard refinement

## Included work units

- WU-034, WU-035, WU-036, and WU-038 through WU-044

## Changed

- Every meeting now has a durable `/meetings/[ID]` workspace with its timing, lifecycle, required or optional status, recurrence, notes, attendance closing time, meeting switcher, attendance roster, corrections, excuses, manual status changes, clear actions, manual refresh, and 30-second refresh while attendance remains open. Legacy Attendance links redirect to the matching meeting detail or Dashboard.
- The top-right contest notifier opens an accessible review popup with member IDs, meeting occurrence dates, reason validation, resolution feedback, and an immediately refreshed pending count.
- Roster and Reports member links now update the displayed route reliably, including direct loads and browser Back/Forward navigation. Member detail conditionally shows the paired Discord ID or a muted unlinked state while Discord is enabled.
- Admins can explicitly enable or disable Google, Resend, and Discord. Disabled providers remain compact, expose no configuration form, and cannot power sign-in, delivery, calendar, pairing, contest, or status workflows. Existing saved providers remain enabled during upgrade, and Google retains its sole-sign-in lockout protection.
- Reports explains operational baseline versus preserved history and keeps selected values and arrows visible. Roster rows align their values and actions, kiosk replacement sits with physical kiosk information, routine kiosk-refresh success is hidden, and the light/dark control is an accessible persistent toggle.
- Release verification uses isolated browser-test ports and bounded, transient-only dependency-audit retries so candidate validation remains resilient without delaying or reducing high-severity security findings.

## Upgrade impact

- Migration `0021_integration_enablement.sql` adds persisted enablement flags for Google, Resend, and Discord. Existing integrations with saved credentials remain enabled; new or unconfigured providers default to disabled. Credentials stay encrypted and verification requirements remain unchanged.
- No new secrets, deployment credentials, environment configuration, or Raspberry Pi changes are required.
- Existing `/attendance?meetingId=<ID>` bookmarks redirect to `/meetings/<ID>`. Bare `/attendance` redirects to Dashboard.

## Before upgrading

1. Download an **Entire installation** backup from **Settings → Updates**.
2. Run the guarded private-repository upgrade workflow using **Latest stable** and the required confirmation.
3. Schedule any physical kiosk update separately during a maintenance window if a later kiosk-bearing release requires it.

## Manual verification after upgrade

1. Open current, upcoming, late-window, and past meetings from Dashboard and confirm each `/meetings/[ID]` page shows the expected lifecycle and meeting details.
2. Exercise attendance refresh, a reasoned correction, browser Back/Forward, a legacy Attendance bookmark, and the meeting switcher as both Admin and Operator.
3. Review an attendance contest from the top-right notifier and confirm its context, resolution feedback, and count refresh.
4. In **Settings → Integrations**, confirm enabled providers are expanded above compact disabled providers. Verify disabling a provider hides its operational actions, and confirm Google cannot remove the only usable sign-in method.
5. Confirm Discord identity visibility on member detail, Reports selector readability, Roster and Kiosks action placement, and theme-toggle persistence at desktop and mobile widths.

## Known limits

- Automated verification uses local D1, mocked providers, and simulated kiosk transport. This release does not deploy an adopter installation or update a Raspberry Pi.
- The broader Dashboard meeting-browser consolidation, in-place meeting creation and management, meeting-local Discord actions, and displaced-utility cleanup remain planned follow-on work in WU-045 through WU-049.
