# LancerLogin v0.9.0 — Unattended kiosk operations

This release restores the physical kiosk as a full-screen, unattended attendance appliance and moves lifecycle and recovery work into the dedicated dashboard Kiosks page. It upgrades an adopter-owned LancerLogin installation in place and never connects to or migrates a pre-LancerLogin attendance system.

## What changed

- Meeting attendance windows can no longer overlap. Validation includes the organization-wide late-scan allowance and covers one-time creation, recurring series, occurrence and future-series edits, and cutoff changes.
- Fingerprint scans no longer need a typed meeting ID. The Worker chooses the one eligible meeting from the scan timestamp and returns the member, meeting, and arrival/departure result.
- The Pi continuously polls the R503 and provides full-screen ready, processing, welcome, goodbye, unknown-finger, rejected, reader-offline, and cloud-offline states. The reader aura light follows the same semantic states.
- Organization name, subtitle, logo, logo contrast treatment, and primary/secondary colors sync to an owner-only local cache so the kiosk remains branded while offline.
- Scans enter an atomic local queue before delivery and retry in order. Expected 4xx attendance rejections are shown to the member and removed instead of blocking later scans.
- Holding the network indicator for three seconds opens PIN-protected touch Wi-Fi controls. The page lists visible networks, supplies an on-screen keyboard, and never stores or displays the entered password.
- Holding the organization logo/name for three seconds opens PIN-protected fingerprint maintenance. It loads active roster labels from the authenticated installation, tests the reader, guides two-scan enrollment, labels fingers, suggests slots, confirms replacements, and edits local mappings without exporting a template.
- The dashboard Kiosks page now owns add/replace pairing, rename, retire, history, heartbeat, reader state, pending scans, last sync, installed release, maintenance guidance, display reload, software restart, Pi reboot, and local-PIN reset.
- The guided installer configures the serial link before every start, adds the service account to `dialout`, refuses to take port 8788 from another service, verifies local health after install/upgrade, and prints useful service diagnostics on failure.

## Data migrations and security

Migrations `0008_kiosk_operations.sql` and `0009_kiosk_commands.sql` add operational kiosk health fields and an installation-scoped fixed-command queue. Existing roster, meetings, attendance, integrations, users, settings, and kiosk credentials are preserved.

Kiosk commands are Admin-only, device-scoped, expire from polling after 15 minutes, and are limited to four enumerated actions. There is no remote shell, command string, argument payload, or dashboard access to the Pi filesystem. Reboot permission is granted through a narrow Polkit rule only to the `lancerlogin` service account. Network and fingerprint operational endpoints remain loopback-only; LAN access remains limited to setup assets, health, and first pairing.

Fingerprint templates and raw scans remain exclusively inside the R503. The Pi stores only a sensor-slot-to-roster-ID mapping with a finger label. D1 and cloud requests receive identifiers, scan event IDs, timestamps, and scrubbed operational health—never biometric material or Wi-Fi passwords.

## Before upgrading

1. Download an **Entire installation** backup from **Settings → Updates**.
2. Confirm existing meetings do not overlap once the organization-wide late-scan allowance is included. New writes will reject a conflict instead of guessing which meeting should receive a scan.
3. In the private deployment repository, manually run **Install or upgrade LancerLogin**, choose **Upgrade**, keep **Latest stable** selected, enter the existing installation slug, and type the required confirmation.
4. After the dashboard reports v0.9.0, download and run the v0.9.0 guided installer on the Pi. The cloud upgrade does not remotely replace Pi files.
5. If another kiosk service still owns port 8788, stop or uninstall that service yourself, then rerun the installer. LancerLogin reports the conflict and does not disable unrelated software.

The v0.9.0 installer upgrades `/opt/lancerlogin` in place and retains owner-only pairing, branding, queue, mapping, and local-PIN files under `/var/lib/lancerlogin`.

## Manual verification after upgrade

1. On Meetings, create a harmless future meeting and confirm an overlapping one is rejected near the form. Repeat with a recurring occurrence if your schedule uses recurrence.
2. On the Pi, confirm Chromium opens the branded full-screen kiosk and the footer reports reader and cloud state.
3. Scan an enrolled finger twice during an eligible test meeting. Confirm the first result welcomes/checks in and the second says goodbye/checks out, with no meeting selection on the Pi.
4. Disconnect the network, scan once, reconnect, and confirm the pending count returns to zero and attendance appears once in the dashboard.
5. Hold the network indicator for three seconds, create or enter the local PIN, view Wi-Fi networks, and close the protected page without exposing a saved password.
6. Hold the brand for three seconds, unlock maintenance, test the reader, and review slot mappings. Use a nonessential slot for enrollment acceptance.
7. From Kiosks, verify health fields, rename the device, and test **Reload display**. Test restart, reboot, and PIN reset only when a brief attendance interruption is acceptable.
8. Confirm an Operator can monitor kiosk status but cannot issue recovery commands, replace/retire hardware, or enter protected settings.

## Known limits

- The expanded browser simulator remains deferred. Its future implementation must use the same screen and attendance state machine as the physical kiosk, replacing only the fingerprint input boundary and retaining simulator-origin audit data.
- LancerLogin still supports one active physical kiosk per installation. Replacement is explicit and preserves retired device history; multi-kiosk operation remains future work.
- Captive-portal automation is unsupported. Use Ethernet or complete portal sign-in outside LancerLogin.
- Existing overlapping meetings are not rewritten automatically. Adjust them manually before changing the organization-wide late-scan allowance.
