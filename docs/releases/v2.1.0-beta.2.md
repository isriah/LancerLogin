# LancerLogin v2.1.0-beta.2

Labels can now sync both ways with their Discord roles, and kiosks refuse scans that can't be right and tell members when their fingerprint should be re-enrolled. Debug mode is now a per-device Admin switch, with kiosk diagnostics grouped under Debug tools. The meetings calendar always has a selected day, and meetings keep their clock times across daylight-saving changes.

## Features added

- **Two-way Discord label sync.** Each label linked to a Discord role can sync **Dashboard to Discord**, as before, or **Two-way**. Two-way labels are checked every 5 minutes: a role added or removed in Discord updates the label, and label history shows the change as **From Discord**. When both sides change, the dashboard wins. A label's Discord role, its sync direction and its action now sit together in the Discord role cell on **Settings > Modules > Attendance > Labels**.
- **Alphabetical Discord roles.** Discord role pickers list roles by name.
- **Impossible scans are refused.** A fingerprint match on a different kiosk less than 30 seconds after the same member's accepted scan is not recorded, and the kiosk shows **Scan again**. Kiosks on older releases record these scans as before.
- **Re-enrollment prompts.** When a member's recent fingerprint scores stay low, the kiosk tells them to ask a mentor to re-enroll their fingerprint, and still records their attendance. Re-enrolling from the dashboard or on the kiosk's maintenance screen clears the prompt.
- **Debug mode per device.** The **Debug** switch in the Settings bar is Admin-only and stored in this browser, so it is off on every new device. The **Developer options** card is gone.
- **Kiosk Debug tools.** With Debug mode on, the **Kiosks** page groups rejected scans, fingerprint matching, possible wrong matches, members who should re-enroll, and Discord users who hold a label role but aren't on the roster.
- **Accounts and roles.** Accounts are listed in a table with their sign-in, role and modules, Admins first. **Grant access** adds an account, and inactive accounts are grouped at the bottom.
- **One Modules card.** **Settings > Modules** lists every module on one card with its switch, source and version, and the dashboard says "module" instead of "plugin". Add, update and remove are applied from the same card. **Settings > Updates** is for releases only.
- **Meetings calendar.** A day is always selected: today when the page opens, and the 1st when you change months. **Add meeting** starts the new meeting on the selected day. On desktop, clicking a day with one meeting opens that meeting.
- **Backups** keep where each label change came from and each label's sync direction. A two-way label comes back from a restore paused until an Admin reviews its differences.

## Bugs fixed

- Moving, duplicating or repeating a meeting keeps its clock times across a daylight-saving change, so a 7 PM to 9 AM meeting stays 7 PM to 9 AM. Duplicates that ended in the repeated 1 AM hour no longer come out an hour short.
- The **Add member**, **Import** and **Bulk edit** labels on the Roster are centered in their buttons.
- The Roster filters line up on one row.
- Contest cards no longer show an arrow after the member ID.

## Features removed

- The **Developer options** card. Use the **Debug** switch in the Settings bar.
- The separate **Plugins** card on **Settings > Modules**.

Updating also dismisses any scan reviews that are still open, because rejected-scan review is now a Debug tools task.
