# LancerLogin v2.1.0-beta.3

Any touchscreen laptop or tablet can now be an attendance kiosk. Pair it from the Kiosks page, and members check in by typing their member ID or searching for their name, with an optional member PIN. Web kiosks replace the browser simulator. A new FIRST connection on Settings > Integrations looks up FRC, FTC and FLL teams by number. Pi kiosks ask members to scan again after a faint fingerprint read and recover from Reader offline on their own, and meetings open in a new tab on Ctrl-click.

## Bugs fixed

- After a failed fingerprint read, a kiosk that showed **Reader offline** returns to its ready screen as soon as the reader works again. Before, it stayed on **Reader offline** until the next member scanned.
- A faint, smudged or unusable fingerprint read shows **Scan again** with a hint, such as pressing a little firmer or wiping the reader, instead of **Reader offline**. Nothing is recorded. A reader that doesn't answer still shows **Reader offline**.
- When an absence notice is cleaned up after its contest window, or withdrawn after a meeting edit, its **Attendance questions** thread in Discord is deleted with it. Threads from notices that were cleaned up before this release stay; delete them by hand.
- Ctrl-click, Cmd-click and middle-click on a meeting in the meetings calendar or the meetings table open it in a new tab. A plain click still opens it in place.
- Outreach activity titles no longer break mid-word in a narrow first column. The other columns fit their content, and the table no longer scrolls sideways in a 960-pixel-wide window.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons are the same size across the dashboard. Radios such as **Meeting audience** are round instead of stretched, and the **Attendance required** check mark is centered.

## Features added

- **Web kiosks.** A touchscreen laptop or tablet can run your dashboard site's kiosk page as an attendance kiosk. On **Kiosks**, an Admin chooses **Add kiosk**, then **Web kiosk**, enters a **Kiosk name** and chooses **Create pairing code**. On the device, scan the QR code, or open the link and enter the 8-character code. The code works once, within 10 minutes. The device then chooses its own settings PIN for its maintenance menu, which opens when you hold the organization name for 3 seconds.
  - Web kiosks keep working offline. Scans are saved on the device and sent in order when the network returns, and they use the server's time, so a wrong device clock doesn't move attendance.
  - The kiosk page can be installed as an app. The [kiosk guide](https://isriah.github.io/LancerLogin/kiosk.html) lists the steps for each platform.
  - From **Manage kiosk**, a web kiosk takes **Reload display** and **Reset settings PIN**.
  - The Kiosks page labels each kiosk **Raspberry Pi** or **Web kiosk**.
- **Retire and replace.** When you pair a kiosk under the name of one that hasn't checked in for 10 minutes, **Add kiosk** offers **Retire and replace**. It retires the old kiosk and keeps its attendance history.
- **Member ID and Member search.** **Manage kiosk > Identification** sets how members identify themselves: **Member ID** on a keypad, or **Member search** by name, ID or email. Search results show only names and IDs. Members confirm their name before anything is recorded. Pi kiosks set to **Member ID entry** get both modes and the confirm step once they are updated to this release.
- **Member PINs.** **Require PIN** under **Identification** asks members for a 4 to 6 digit PIN after they confirm their name.
  - A member without a PIN taps **Ask for approval** on the kiosk, and chooses a PIN once an Operator approves it from **PIN requests** on **Manage kiosk**. **Set member PIN** in the kiosk's maintenance menu sets one directly.
  - The **Kiosk PIN** card on a member's page has **Reset PIN** and **Unlock**.
  - Kiosks also check PINs while offline.
- **Kiosk PIN lockout.** On **Settings > Modules > Attendance > Meeting configuration**, an Admin can lock a member's PIN for a set number of minutes after repeated wrong tries. It is off by default.
- **Attendance setup pairs a real kiosk.** The **Verified attendance input** step has its own **Add kiosk** button and completes once a paired Pi or web kiosk checks in. Kiosks can pair while setup is still running.
- **Open web kiosk on this device.** With Debug mode on, an Admin can open a web kiosk in a new tab from **Debug tools** on **Kiosks**, to try it without a second device.
- **Meeting links on member pages.** Each meeting in **Complete attendance history** on a member's page links to that meeting.
- **FIRST team lookup.** **Settings > Integrations** has a **FIRST** card, so modules can look up teams by number. Only the team number is sent to FIRST.
  - **FRC** and **FTC** use a FIRST Events API **Username** and **Token**. LancerLogin checks them with FIRST before saving them.
  - **FLL** (Challenge and Explore) uses FIRST's public team search and needs no setup.
  - **Test a team** looks up one team. LancerLogin keeps a found team for 7 days and a missing one for 1 day, and **Clear cached answers** makes the next lookups ask FIRST again.
- **Backups.** Entire-installation backups include each kiosk's kind, identification and **Require PIN** setting, member PIN hashes, the PIN lockout setting, and the FIRST usernames and encrypted tokens. Kiosks from older backups restore as Raspberry Pi kiosks.
- **Module SDK 1.2.0.** `services.first` lets modules look up FIRST teams through the installation's FIRST connection. The SDK also exports `AppLink`, `followInApp`, `followRowInApp` and `isModifiedClick`, for links that open in a new tab on Ctrl-click.

## Features removed

- The browser simulator: the **Browser simulator** page, its card on **Kiosks**, and its use in Attendance setup. Pair a web kiosk instead.

## Updating

Before or with this update, give the LancerLogin Discord bot the **Manage Threads** permission in the attendance channel. Without it, expired absence notices keep retrying their cleanup, and a notice withdrawn by a meeting edit stays waiting to be deleted.

Updating removes the browser simulator's saved data. Backups made before this release drop it when restored.
